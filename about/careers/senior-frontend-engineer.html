Senior Frontend Engineer

Engineering team Remote (EU time zones) Full-time Competitive · equity for senior hires
Apply for this role

About the role

Own the front end of products founders bet on.

You'll be the senior front-end voice on a small delivery team — usually two to four engineers, a designer, and a delivery lead — building products for non-technical founders and scale-ups. That means turning ambiguous briefs into interfaces people trust with their money and their time, and setting the front-end bar the rest of the team builds against.

You won't be handed pixel-perfect specs and a ticket queue. You'll sit close to the problem, push back when something's the wrong thing to build, and ship weekly. When we hand a codebase back to a client's team, yours is the code they should be able to pick up and keep going.

What you'll do

  • Lead front-end architecture on one or two concurrent product builds.
  • Turn Figma designs into accessible, performant React/Next.js interfaces.
  • Set patterns — component libraries, state, testing — the team reuses.
  • Review PRs and mentor mid-level engineers.
  • Talk directly to clients about trade-offs, not through a middle layer.

What we're looking for

  • 5+ years building production web apps, senior for at least two.
  • Deep React + TypeScript; comfortable across a Next.js app end-to-end.
  • An eye for design and a habit of caring about accessibility and performance.
  • Judgment about what to build now versus what can wait.
  • Clear communication with non-technical stakeholders.

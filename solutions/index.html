Why we build products

Shipping our own products makes us better at shipping yours.

Running real products — with real users, real uptime and real churn — keeps the same team that builds for clients honest about the things that actually matter after launch.

We live with our decisions

Every shortcut we'd be tempted to take, we've paid for in our own on-call rotation. That judgment goes straight into client work.

Patterns, proven twice

Onboarding, billing, moderation, search — we've solved them for ourselves, so we don't bill you to discover them from scratch.

Buy vs. build, answered

Sometimes the fastest path is a product we already run. We'll tell you when that's true instead of quoting a custom build.

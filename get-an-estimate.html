What happens after you submit

Three steps, no mystery.

An estimate is a starting point, not a contract. Here's exactly how we get you a useful one — and what turns a ballpark into a firm number.

  1. 1

    We read it

    A real person on the team — not a bot — reads what you sent and works out what you're really after. If something's unclear, we'll ask a quick question or two.

  2. 2

    You get a ballpark by email

    An honest range — wide enough that we'd stand behind it, narrow enough to plan around — with a timeline and the engagement model we'd suggest. A clear brief tightens it; scope that's still taking shape widens it.

  3. 3

    Optional call, if you want one

    Want to talk it through? Book 30 minutes with a scoping lead and we'll turn the range into a sequenced plan. Not ready? There's no follow-up you didn't ask for.
